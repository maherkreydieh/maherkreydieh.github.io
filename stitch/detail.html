In a wide ceramic dish, vigorously whisk yogurt, cold-pressed olive oil, fresh lemon juice, crushed garlic, tomato paste, sumac, and Lebanese 7-spices until smooth and deeply fragrant.

tips_and_updatesPro Tip: Lemon juice acid combined with yogurt cultures naturally tenderizes the lean breast meat.

Fold in the chicken cubes, ensuring every facet is richly coated. Cover tightly and refrigerate for a minimum of 4 hours, or ideally overnight, allowing the aromas of garlic and spices to deeply saturate.

3
Skewer & Charcoal Searoutdoor_grill 15 min

Thread chicken onto flat stainless or soaked wooden skewers, alternating with red onion petals. Sear over high lump charcoal or screaming hot cast iron for 12–15 minutes, turning regularly until caramelized with tender juicy centers.

About Akletna Heritage

Tawook is rooted in the tradition of mountain gathering and Sunday outdoor grills across Mount Lebanon. The hallmark of true Tawook is never dry meat; the garlic-acid brine locks in moisture even under intense charcoal fire.
